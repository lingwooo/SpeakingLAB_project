The Most Effective Strategies for Improving IELTS Speaking Skills Among Teenagers

A student research project exploring how different learning strategies can improve fluency, pronunciation, vocabulary and confidence among teenage IELTS learners.

Research Question

What actually helps teenagers improve their IELTS Speaking?

🎯

Research Aim

To identify which learning strategies can produce noticeable improvements in IELTS Speaking performance.

🧪

Experiment

Teenagers can be divided into groups and use different speaking strategies for a fixed period of time.

📊

Measurement

Participants can complete speaking tasks before and after the experiment and compare their results.

4 Strategies

The strategies tested in this research

STRATEGY 01

🎧 Shadowing

Students listen to native or advanced speakers and repeat their speech while copying pronunciation, rhythm and intonation.

Pronunciation + Fluency
STRATEGY 02

🤖 AI Speaking

Students practise speaking with an AI assistant, receive instant feedback and learn how to improve their answers.

Feedback + Vocabulary
STRATEGY 03

👥 Speaking Clubs

Students regularly communicate with classmates and practise IELTS questions in a social environment.

Confidence + Fluency
STRATEGY 04

🎙️ Self-recording

Students record their own answers, listen again, identify mistakes and repeat the task.

Self-correction

A possible experimental design for teenage IELTS learners

How the experiment works

Participants complete the same speaking task before and after several weeks of practice. Each group uses one main strategy.

Research Conclusion

What this project aims to discover

The key question

Instead of assuming that one strategy is the best, this project compares several approaches using measurable Speaking results.

The final conclusion should be based on the actual pre-test and post-test data collected from participants.

This can help teenagers understand which learning techniques are most useful for improving their IELTS Speaking skills.
